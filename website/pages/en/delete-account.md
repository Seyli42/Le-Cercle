# Delete your DoseCircle account

## From the app (immediate)

1. Open DoseCircle and tap **Account** (top right of the home screen).
2. Tap **Delete my account**.
3. Type **DELETE** to confirm, then **Delete permanently**.

An internet connection is needed. Beforehand, you can **Export my data**.

## Without the app

Write to [{{contactEmail}}](mailto:{{contactEmail}}?subject=Account%20deletion)
**from your account's email address**, with the subject “Account deletion”. We delete the
account and confirm it within 7 days at most.

## What is deleted

- Immediately and permanently: your account, your medications, times, dose history, your
  Circle (links with your loved ones, phones registered for alerts), the alert log, and
  your purchase history at our subscription provider.
- Our host's technical backups expire within 7 days.
- Only the invoices kept by Apple or Google, under their legal obligations, remain with
  them.

**Premium subscription:** deleting the account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it
in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
