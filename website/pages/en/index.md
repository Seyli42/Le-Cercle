# DoseCircle

**Your medication, on time. Your loved ones, reassured.**

DoseCircle reminds you of every dose, even offline, with the phone locked or after a
restart. If a dose isn't confirmed, the loved ones you chose are alerted by a notification
on their phone.

{{downloadLinks}}

## What the app does

- Reliable reminders, with “Taken”, “In 10 min” or “Skip” right from the notification.
- My Circle: a child, a neighbour, a home carer alerted if a dose is missed. They install
  DoseCircle for free and enter your code.
- Dose history, big buttons, dark mode.
- Available in 11 languages.
- Free with a few non-personalised ads, or Premium without ads.

> DoseCircle is not a medical device: the app reminds you of what you entered, it gives no
> advice. If in doubt, ask your doctor or pharmacist.
