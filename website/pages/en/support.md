# Help and contact

## My reminders don't ring

In the app: **Account → Check my reminders**. The screen shows what is blocking
(notifications refused, battery saver, exact alarms on Android) with a button to fix it.

## Premium

- **Restore a purchase** (new phone): Account → DoseCircle Premium → Restore my purchases,
  with the same App Store / Google Play account.
- **Cancel**: your App Store or Google Play account settings → Subscriptions.

## My loved one no longer wants to be alerted

In their app: **My Circle → Stop looking out**. You can also remove them yourself:
**My Circle → Remove**.

## My loved one doesn't receive the alerts

On their phone, DoseCircle notifications must be allowed, and they must be signed in to
their account (signing out stops the alerts on that phone).
